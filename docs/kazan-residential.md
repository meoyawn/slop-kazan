# Unified Kazan residential housing

The active repair separates architectural type from construction material. `tools/kazan-residential.mjs` is the production entry point: source-backed panel structure, compact first-generation housing, Stalinka architecture and brick housing resolve to one final `housingType`. A panel Khrushchev house has `type=khrushchev` and `structure=panel-bearing`; continuous high-rise loggias do not follow from its material. Unknown evidence remains unknown.

`tools/kazan-housing.mjs` joins exact unique municipal addresses, preserving street type, suffix, corpus and typed OSM identity. The original843-card Domivita filter is useful positive material evidence but misses real panels recorded as `Иные`. The new Prawdom importer follows all advertised Kazan street-index pages, actual house links and mapped residential addresses. It retains both passport sections, construction versus commissioning, contradictory wall/project facts and absent balcony counts. Regional series pages explicitly show at most50 sample houses and are not used as a census. Full provenance and raw structural facts stay in the saved tools dataset; HTML/scripts/resident facts are not shipped.

Generator rules live in `tools/data/kazan-building-observations.json`. Brick housing has ordered period rules for2000s,2010s,2020s and earlier/unknown construction. They select shared window, loggia, end-wall, paint-zone and plinth parameters; adding a period variant does not require an address branch or a new renderer. Existing reviewed multicolour facades, models, Stalinka crowns/sections, historical envelopes and specialist systems retain ownership. Literal mapped colours override estimates. Unmeasured finishes use clean, attractive local palettes, not invented renovation/seam data.

The compact Khrushchev estimate combines local construction-period evidence with apartment use, plan occupancy, dimensions, storey proportions, roof and available project evidence. Transition-period houses need a project signal before taking precedence over Stalinka rules. A roof alone or a universal floor/year cutoff does not establish the class. Broad/source-conflicting projects are retained explicitly. Small balcony railings, stair-landing windows and mullions reuse the existing facade shader and packed distant representation; they add no individual balcony meshes or lights.
