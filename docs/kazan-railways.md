# Kazan heavy rail

The railway system uses the same regional OSM France extract as the city, timestamped 2026-10-21. `tools/import-kazan-osm.py` retains railway ways and nodes; `tools/kazan-railways.mjs` compiles a separate railway graph. Rail links never enter the ambient motor graph. Abandoned, proposed and disused ways, trams and the metro are excluded from this heavy-rail renderer. The mapped children's narrow-gauge line remains distinct through its railway kind and gauge.

The current import contains 939 track ways, approximately 571.3 track-kilometres, 43 stations/halts, 66 platforms and 1,251 switches, crossings, buffers and signals. It includes both passenger terminals, Yudino's yard, the airport branch and the eastern edge of the Rus-German Switzerland forest (`way/1320493492`, continuing north as `way/36264604`). Those forest-edge tracks are separate from the rail tunnel farther south (`way/41729835`).

OSM node identity determines junctions. Lines crossing geometrically never create a junction by proximity. Each way represents one track unless an explicit `tracks` tag says otherwise. The compiler retains gauge, track reference/count, service/yard use, access, operator, maximum speed, electrification, voltage, frequency, direction, bridge, tunnel and layer. A missing gauge uses a labelled regional estimate (1,520 mm main line or 750 mm narrow gauge). Missing electrification stays unknown and does not create overhead equipment. Tagged gauges are present on 789 ways; 358 have mapped contact-line electrification, 332 explicitly have none and 249 are unspecified.

`js/kazan-railways.js` samples one shared arc-length profile for rails, ballast, sleepers, structures and catenary. Shared nodes meet at the same height, with estimated structural clearances and approach grades. Bridge decks/supports and tunnel lining/portals follow their own rail level. The terrain opens at tunnel entrances and retains ground over covered interiors. Trees and distant crowns clear the exposed railway corridor. Fine sleepers and overhead equipment stream by spatial cell; ballast/rails remain visible at distance. Both radar and full map show the rail network separately from streets.

The historic station (`way/81560006`), Kazan-2 (`way/83537498`) and Narimanova 2 (`way/81560021`, visible behind Kazan-1) use shared station facade components over their mapped envelopes. Observed palettes and sources are recorded in `tools/data/kazan-building-observations.json`, including the complete visible-building checklists. References include the [2024 Kazan-1 photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Railway_Station_2024-07-13_1314.jpg), [December 2025 side view](https://domophoto.ru/photo/331448/), [Narimanova 2 detail](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Narimanov_street_2,_Kazan_(2022-10-14)_01.jpg), [Kazan-2 exterior listing](https://yandex.ru/maps/org/kazan_2/50918390495/) and the [2024 event authority arrival guide](https://dspkazan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/%D0%93%D0%98%D0%94_%D0%9F%D0%A0%D0%98%D0%91%D0%AB%D0%A2%D0%98%D0%99_%D0%98_%D0%9E%D0%A2%D0%AA%D0%95%D0%97%D0%94%D0%9E%D0%92_rus_.pdf).

Sleeper spacing, rail sections, structural clearances, station height and ornament sizes are procedural estimates. Exact sculpted heraldry, leopard statues, rear ornament and obscured service doors remain photographic detail follow-ups. No timetable or running train service is claimed by the static railway system.

Verification covers source operational tags, mapped-node topology, connectivity between the main stations/airport/forest corridor, shared profile heights, tunnel entrance cuts and covered interiors. The topology regression fails on the preceding geography because no track network was imported. The profile regression also caught negative-layer approaches being raised and now passes with the corrected signed structural offsets.
