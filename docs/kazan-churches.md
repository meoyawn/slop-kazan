# Photographic church scenes

The first scene is Пятницкая церковь (OSM way/90251716) at Большая Красная, 1/2. The photographic checklist lives in `tools/data/kazan-building-observations.json`, scene `paraskeva-hill-and-west-front`. It remains active until its complete visible background has been addressed.

Reviewed external references are the 13 July 2024 Commons photographs numbered [1400](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Saint_Paraskeva_Pyatnytsya_Church_2024-07-13_1400.jpg), [1406](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Saint_Paraskeva_Pyatnytsya_Church_2024-07-13_1406.jpg), [1463](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Saint_Paraskeva_Pyatnytsya_Church_2024-07-13_1463.jpg) and [1467](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Saint_Paraskeva_Pyatnytsya_Church_2024-07-13_1467.jpg). The [diocesan church record](https://www.tatmitropolia.ru/hramy_tatarstana/kazan_eparhiya/kazan/kazan/?id=44495) identifies the restored belfry, parish administration and memorial shrine. These sources do not provide surveyed elevations.

`js/kazan-churches.js` retains the mapped lower hall, apse, octagonal nave and west bell-tower positions. The parent model replaces the generic stacked church parts. Shared components render plaster cornices, framed/arched windows, sheet-metal caps, onion heads, crosses, an open belfry with a bell, and the four-column west portico. The small Всецарица chapel (way/889403435), memorial shrine (way/1279451019) and parish office (way/90251711) remain separate mapped buildings. The court paving follows sampled rendered terrain. The property and separate barrier lines now follow the regional OSM extract; photographic wall/ironwork profiles retain the mapped entrance breaks and gates.

The opposite historic street row uses shared plaster families with individual cream, ochre and blue-grey palettes and separate brown/green roofs. The eastern five-storey neighbour (way/90251781) keeps panel joints, balcony stacks and its silver gabled roof. The red apartment block at Большая Красная, 8 (way/247787105) retains its distinct five- and eight-storey parts and an inherited red/tan palette.

The palace, cathedral, distant roof fragments, exact mansion dormers and obscured elevations remain explicit checklist items. The church scene is not declared complete by assigning a facade family to those buildings. Component heights, bay spacing, roof sections, rear detail and fence section dimensions are procedural estimates tied to the references; the source OSM footprint identities remain available in the compiled geography.

Recompiling this batch also applies the existing train-only stop filter consistently to the saved tram geography: 210 tram stop/stop-position records remain after excluding two nearby train stops. Track, route and platform counts are unchanged.

The west portico now has separate solid church walls, column bases/shafts and overhead shapes. The central approach stays open. Its landing, terrain-fitted treads and rails share the access layout used by pedestrian surface heights. Step count/run and rail sections are visual/DEM estimates. Regressions fail on the preceding model's solid parent envelope and terrain-only foot heights. Browser controller sweeps through all three centre lanes report zero displacement; nearby columns and the overhead beam still stop the actor. Downward rays hit the rendered treads at the walking surface.

The neighbouring round tower (way/889405857) and administrative building (way/90251745) use [2024 close photographs](https://www.watertowers.ru/drugie-bashni/bashnya-ogrady-kazanskogo-bogoroditskogo-monastyrya). The tower is an open brick shell with remaining limewash, a blind arcade, narrow upper openings and a broken rim. A shared weathered-brick family supplies the masonry. The three-storey office retains pale walls, rectangular lower windows, arched upper windows and a green roof. Its historical context is recorded separately from its current use. The eastern apartment block has separate projecting glazed balconies, spandrels, dividers, dormers and stacks; unseen details remain estimates.
