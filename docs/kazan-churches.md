# Photographic church scenes

The first scene is Пятницкая церковь (OSM way/90251716) at Большая Красная, 1/2. The photographic checklist lives in `tools/data/kazan-building-observations.json`, scene `paraskeva-hill-and-west-front`. It remains active until its complete visible background has been addressed.

Reviewed external references are the 13 July 2024 Commons photographs numbered [1400](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Saint_Paraskeva_Pyatnytsya_Church_2024-07-13_1400.jpg), [1406](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Saint_Paraskeva_Pyatnytsya_Church_2024-07-13_1406.jpg), [1463](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Saint_Paraskeva_Pyatnytsya_Church_2024-07-13_1463.jpg) and [1467](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Saint_Paraskeva_Pyatnytsya_Church_2024-07-13_1467.jpg). The [diocesan church record](https://www.tatmitropolia.ru/hramy_tatarstana/kazan_eparhiya/kazan/kazan/?id=44495) identifies the restored belfry, parish administration and memorial shrine. These sources do not provide surveyed elevations.

`js/kazan-churches.js` retains the mapped lower hall, apse, octagonal nave and west bell-tower positions. The parent model replaces the generic stacked church parts. Shared components render plaster cornices, framed/arched windows, sheet-metal caps, onion heads, crosses, an open belfry with a bell, and the four-column west portico. The small Всецарица chapel (way/889403435), memorial shrine (way/1279451019) and parish office (way/90251711) remain separate mapped buildings. The court paving follows sampled rendered terrain. Estimated perimeter sections retain openings at the gates; their appearance follows the photographs and their coordinates are explicitly labelled as estimates, rather than OSM boundary observations.

The opposite historic street row uses shared plaster families with individual cream, ochre and blue-grey palettes and separate brown/green roofs. The eastern five-storey neighbour (way/90251781) keeps panel joints, balcony stacks and its silver gabled roof. The red apartment block at Большая Красная, 8 (way/247787105) retains its distinct five- and eight-storey parts and an inherited red/tan palette.

The palace, cathedral, ruined round tower, distant roof fragments, exact mansion dormers and obscured elevations remain explicit checklist items. The church scene is not declared complete by assigning a facade family to those buildings. Component heights, bay spacing, roof sections, rear detail and fence positions are procedural estimates tied to the references; the source OSM footprint identities remain available in the compiled geography.

Recompiling this batch also applies the existing train-only stop filter consistently to the saved tram geography: 210 tram stop/stop-position records remain after excluding two nearby train stops. Track, route and platform counts are unchanged.
