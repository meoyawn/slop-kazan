# Procedural sports venues

Shared indoor arenas and open football stadiums use separate procedural families. Basket Hall is published; Ak Bars Arena is the current checked exterior batch. Each venue retains its mapped footprint and component geometry. No additional KAI academic work is queued.

## Inventory and ownership

`tools/import-kazan-sports.py` supplements the existing city extract from the identical SHA-paired local regional PBF. The base import omitted most stadium properties, rinks and sports POIs. The supplement retains2,454 raw sports/context records and reports zero incomplete sports relations. Raw records are not a count of physical venues.

`tools/kazan-sports.mjs` deduplicates explicit relation/perimeter aliases and POIs within physical sites. The regional inventory contains257 sites,238 inside the municipal boundary,295 source identities,314 parent/component building bindings and103 mapped parts. There are39 mapped sports properties;218 sites have no established property boundary. These unknown boundaries are retained, not invented around a nearby building.1,217 source ground features are retained;1,153 have no established sports-property owner. Many belong to school/community grounds, already handled by their own subsystem.

Whole-footprint polygon intersection checks prevent a property claiming a boundary-crossing building, a foreign body inside a courtyard hole or a footprint spanning a hole despite having all its corners outside it. Independent fitness POIs inside commercial/residential buildings keep a shared-envelope binding and their ordinary facade. Multi-sport tags survive classification. Context is separate from venue size:31 sites lie in school grounds and4 in university grounds. A campus hall is not automatically a major spectator arena. Inventory counts describe mapped physical sites, not a verified institutional census or delivered rendering coverage.
