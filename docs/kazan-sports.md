# Procedural sports venues

Active implementation: shared indoor arenas, starting with Basket Hall. Open football stadiums will use a separate pitch/stands/bowl/canopy family. Each venue retains its mapped footprint and component geometry. No additional KAI academic work is queued.

## Inventory and ownership

`tools/import-kazan-sports.py` supplements the existing city extract from the identical SHA-paired local regional PBF. The base import omitted most stadium properties, rinks and sports POIs. The supplement retains2,454 raw sports/context records and reports zero incomplete sports relations. Raw records are not a count of physical venues.

`tools/kazan-sports.mjs` deduplicates explicit relation/perimeter aliases and POIs within physical sites. The regional inventory contains257 sites,238 inside the municipal boundary,295 source identities,314 parent/component building bindings and103 mapped parts. There are39 mapped sports properties;218 sites have no established property boundary. These unknown boundaries are retained, not invented around a nearby building.1,217 source ground features are retained;1,153 have no established sports-property owner. Many belong to school/community grounds, already handled by their own subsystem.

Whole-footprint polygon intersection checks prevent a property claiming a boundary-crossing building, a foreign body inside a courtyard hole or a footprint spanning a hole despite having all its corners outside it. Independent fitness POIs inside commercial/residential buildings keep a shared-envelope binding and their ordinary facade. Multi-sport tags survive classification. Context is separate from venue size:31 sites lie in school grounds and4 in university grounds. A campus hall is not automatically a major spectator arena. Inventory counts describe mapped physical sites, not a verified institutional census or delivered rendering coverage.

## Basket Hall

Reviewed sources: [UNICS](https://unics.ru/about/baskethall/), [municipal tourism gallery credited to UNICS](https://go.kzn.ru/places/basket-xoll), and [the builder](https://odakgroup.com.tr/basket-hall-3). The builder confirms an octagonal main hall, auxiliary hall and connecting passage. UNICS lists7,000 seats in the main hall and1,500 in the small hall; the builder's training-hall capacity refers to a different arrangement and is not substituted for these figures.

Parent way/504510364 is rendered from its actual parts: main octagonal hall way/172280394, small hall way/172280393 and connector way/172280407. The main total height24.3m uses the existing parent GBA scale as a photographic estimate;16.5m eaves and7.8m roof rise are procedural estimates. Original four-storey and roof tags remain intact as source evidence. Small hall9m/three mapped storeys and connector6.4m retain their source massing. The northwest entrance is tied to node/9760243362 and the actual beveled wall, with an estimated24m-wide,3.2m-deep,1.2m-rise stair of eight treads. No property boundary or car-free campus is invented from a building outline.

`js/kazan-sports.js` shares an indoor hall envelope for mapped basketball/hockey buildings: pale cladding/piers, broad glazing, fascia and source-sized roof. Basket Hall adds its estimated faceted roof, basketball sculptures and one cached facade sign. Geometry joins the existing spatial city batches without a per-arena group/flush, lights, interior, animation or facade textures. Existing reviewed landmark renderers, including Tatneft Arena, take precedence. School/university halls and shared fitness envelopes retain their existing appearance until their appropriate sports variant is checked.

Collision uses the three actual component footprints/heights, preserving the low connector and small hall. The stairs share their layout with the walking-surface service. Narrow foundation strips meet the terrain along the walls; there is no broad artificial platform.
