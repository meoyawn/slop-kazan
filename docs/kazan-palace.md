# Palace of Farmers exterior

The existing palace is OSM way/90251771. Its central roof is part way/295851849; four corner roofs retain 295851851, 295851854, 295851857 and 295851859. The parent replaces the former four-level generic extrusion and the low roof-part fallbacks.

The [ministry's 2024 record](https://agro.tatarstan.ru/eng/index.htm/news/2364531.htm) identifies the two symmetrical wings, bronze 20 m tree and spiral lattice dome. The [research institute's event page](https://biz.gosniihp.ru/tpost/a4f69x40y1-delovaya-chast-meropriyatiya-proidet-vo) gives 48 m overall height. Reviewed photographs: [central facade, 19 September 2020](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Central_facade_of_the_Palace_of_Farmers_in_Kazan,_19_September_2020.jpg) and [corner view, 13 July 2024](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kazan_Agriculturers_Palace_2024-07-13_1466.jpg). They establish cream limestone, a rusticated base, window tiers, columns, balustrades, the arched glazed recess and dark metal caps. The central dome is open geometry with spiral ribs and connecting rings; the corner caps have separate open lanterns.

The foundation uses the centre-front terrain sample. Bodies extend below the terrain while mapped drive-through cuts stay in both geometry and collision shapes. The central landing and terrain-fitted flight share their rendered/walkable step layout. Bay spacing, tier dimensions, bronze branch topology, window muntins and staircase dimensions are visual or DEM estimates. The 20 m tree is a procedural branching sculpture, with green night illumination, not a botanical tree with foliage.

The complete scene is not declared finished: sculptural horses/winged bars, exact reliefs/capitals, rear elevations, square planting, adjacent apartment identities and the brick ruin fragment remain explicit follow-ups in `farmers-palace-central-and-corner-fronts`. These must not hold the sequential queue on repeated palace-polishing batches while the other requested objects wait.
