# Procedural worship buildings

This is one citywide generator task, not a queue of individual reconstructions. `tools/kazan-worship.mjs` classifies saved mapped footprints using explicit Muslim identity/mosque/minaret tags and Orthodox denominations. Unknown Christian denominations, other religions, shared occupied envelopes and religiously affiliated schools retain their existing use and appearance. Full-footprint intersection assigns monastery components only inside actual mapped property outlines and outside holes. Source dimensions, heights, parts, colours and every existing geography field remain unchanged; the separate `worship` inventory records family inference and identity gaps. Counts describe mapped building records, not congregations or delivered photographic scenes.

`js/kazan-worship.js` draws these families directly into existing city tile batches. It combines original body/wing/apse outlines and mapped parts with bounded arched-window rhythms, cornices, pale plaster/red brick/timber palettes, metal roofs, octagonal minarets, drums and onion or rounded domes. Mapped minarets retain their source height/location and suppress a nearby estimated roof minaret without establishing property ownership by proximity. Unmapped minarets and dome arrangements are explicit family estimates. Existing reviewed church/cathedral/monastery models remain in charge of their buildings. No per-building group/flush, photograph textures, new lights or animation are introduced.

Orthodox altar/entrance axes follow the source footprint, choosing its eastward longitudinal direction only as an estimate; existing apses and body orientations remain intact. Portals use the opposite end. This is not a surveyed altar-bearing dataset and does not rotate churches to a universal heading. Single-domed bodies, source-composed churches, source bell towers and a bounded five-dome cathedral variant share components. Muslim variants include pitched-roof halls, domed halls, roof-mounted or mapped independent minarets, timber/brick/plaster walls and shared green/blue metal coatings. Palette values approximate photographed style; they are not calibrated satellite measurements.
